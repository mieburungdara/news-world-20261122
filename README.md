One picture that sums up the world's news of Sunday 22 November 2026: a front page with the date,
six stories drawn as flat pictograms with their region, and three briefs — Iran's strike on a Gulf
industrial plant, Russia's bombardment of Kyiv, the arrest of a Sinaloa cartel boss, a breach of the
FBI's national crime database, the deepening AI sell-off, and BepiColombo's arrival in orbit around
Mercury. The image is laid out in render/poster.html and rendered to entry.png; main.ts checks the
file against the bounty's rules and writes it to the grader's /output.
